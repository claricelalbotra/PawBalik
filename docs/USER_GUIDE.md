# PawBalik User Guide

PawBalik helps people in Tacloban City reunite lost dogs and cats with their owners. It compares photos with Google Gemini and shows **possibly similar** animals with a similarity percentage.

> Similarity percentages are visual indicators only. They do not confirm that this is the same animal or establish ownership. Please verify with identifying details before arranging a handover.

## Getting started

1. Click **Sign Up** and enter your full name, email, Philippine mobile number (e.g. `0917 123 4567`) and a password, and accept the Privacy Notice.
2. To receive SMS match alerts, open **Profile → Verify mobile number**, request a code, and type the 6-digit code you receive.
3. To receive browser push alerts, click **Enable match alerts** and allow notifications.

Guests can browse lost pets and the Rescue Directory. Scanning, reporting and messaging need an account.

## If you lost your pet (owner)

1. **Report a Lost Pet.** Go to **Report a Lost Pet** and complete the 5 steps:
   1. **Pet Information:** type, name, breed, fur color, size, sex, and 1–5 clear photos. The first photo is the one used for matching.
   2. **Identifying Features:** scars, collar, eye color, tail, anything unique.
   3. **Where & When Lost:** tap the map to drop a pin (or use your current location), and give the barangay or landmark and the date and time.
   4. **Contact & Reward:** your contact details, whether to show your phone publicly, and an optional reward.
   5. **Review and Submit.**

   You get a case number such as **PB-2026-000123**. Your report appears in the public list.
2. **Automatic matching.** PawBalik checks your report against private Pet Sightings from finders. If a highly similar sighting exists, now or later, you get an in-app notification, a push alert, and an SMS if your number is verified.
3. **Scan for My Lost Pet.** Go to **Match a Found Pet → Scan for My Lost Pet**, choose your report or upload another photo, and review the ranked sightings. Each shows where and when the animal was seen, with **Open in Google Maps** and **Get Directions** buttons.
4. **Talk to the finder.** Click **Message finder**. Before any handover, follow the **Verification checklist** in the chat: markings, collar or tag, vet records, older photos, details only you would know.
5. **Close your case.** On your case page, click **Mark as Found** when your pet is home, or **Close Case**. Everyone in your conversations is notified, and the status history is kept.

**My Cases** lists all your reports and sightings with their status.

## If you found a pet (finder)

1. Go to **Match a Found Pet**. Choose **Upload Photo** or **Capture Photo**, and allow location if you want results ranked by distance.
2. PawBalik checks the photo, then compares it with lost-pet reports. Results show the similarity %, why the pets look similar or different, the distance, and when the pet was reported lost.
3. If one looks right, click **View Details** or **Message** to contact the owner. In the chat you can **Share location** to send a map pin of where the pet is.
4. If none look right, click **Report as pet sightings**. Pin where you found the pet, add the date and time, its condition, and whether you are holding it. **Sightings are private.** They never appear in public lists. Only owners whose own scans match your sighting can see it.

## If an animal needs help (rescue)

1. Open **Rescue** to see rescuers, veterinary clinics and shelters sorted by distance. You can filter by type, open now, or 24/7.
2. Use **Call**, **Open in Google Maps** or **Get Directions**.
3. From a case or sighting page, click **Request Rescue Help** for a ready-to-copy summary: case number, animal, condition, location link, and contact. Paste it into SMS or Messenger for the provider.

PawBalik does not replace emergency services.

## Admins

Log in with the admin account and open **Admin**:

- **Dashboard:** active and found cases, sightings, scans per day, reunions, and SMS/push activity.
- **Providers:** add, edit or deactivate rescuers, vets and shelters. Replace the SAMPLE entries with verified ones.
- **Reports / Sightings:** hide abusive or spam content with a reason, or unhide it.
- **Users:** search, deactivate or reactivate users. You cannot deactivate yourself or the last admin.
- **Abuse reports:** review "Report this user" submissions.
- **Logs:** audit, SMS and push history.

## Tips for good matches

- Use good light, show the whole body, make the face clearly visible, and have one animal per photo.
- Add several photos from different angles to your report.
- A low percentage does not mean "not your pet", and a high one does not prove it is. Always verify.
