What PawBalik does

PawBalik helps people in Tacloban City, Leyte bring lost dogs and cats back home and find help for animals in need.

  • Owners file a Lost Pet Report and get a case number (for example PB-2026-000123). The report is listed publicly so finders can recognise the pet.
  • Finders scan a photo of a found pet with the Found Pet Scanner. It compares the photo with public lost-pet reports and shows possibly similar animals, so the finder can message the owner.
  • If nothing similar appears, the finder can submit a private Pet Sighting with a location pin. Sightings are never listed publicly. An owner only sees a sighting when their own scan (or our automatic background check) returns it as a possible match.
  • The Rescue Directory lists rescuers, veterinary clinics and shelters with phone numbers and Google Maps directions.

How photo matching works

  1. Photo check. Google Gemini checks that the photo shows exactly one dog or cat and notes the photo quality.
  2. Reading features. Gemini describes stable features: breed guess, coat colours and pattern, size and markings.
  3. Finding candidates. PawBalik looks for reports or sightings of the same species, seen within about 15 km and the last 60 days.
  4. Comparing photos. Gemini compares the photo with each candidate photo and explains matching features and notable differences.
  5. Ranking. A similarity percentage combines the photo comparison (70%), matching details (15%), distance (10%) and how recent the case is (5%).

Limitations

  • Tacloban City only, and domestic dogs and cats only.
  • A similarity percentage is a visual indicator, not proof. People verify identity together using unique markings, collars, vet records and older photos. PawBalik never confirms ownership.
  • Results depend on photo quality: use good lighting, show the whole body and the face, one animal per photo.
  • Case messaging is only for coordinating about a case. {{ S.EMERGENCY_NOTE }}
  • Directions rely on your device's GPS and Google Maps; PawBalik only builds the link.
  • You need an internet connection and a device with a camera or file upload.
